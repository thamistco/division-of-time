# Division of Time

A simple daily time tracker, inspired by Toggl Track and Superfocus. See where your time actually goes.

**Open it:** https://thamistco.github.io/division-of-time/

- One-tap timer with a description and category, or tap a category chip to switch instantly
- Focus sessions (25 / 50 / 90 min) with an end-of-session alert
- Today view: 24-hour timeline, donut breakdown, untracked waking time, goals, editable entries
- Daily goals per category: "at least" (study, family, exercise) or "at most" (admin, phone)
- Daily review: rate the day and jot down what stole your time
- Reports: this week, last week, this month or 30 days, with stacked hours per day, category table, top activities and insights
- Works offline and installs to your phone home screen (PWA); dark mode
- All data stays on your device (localStorage). Export JSON backup or CSV anytime.

Keyboard: `S` start/stop, `N` jump to description.
