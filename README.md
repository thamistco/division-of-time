# Division of Time

A calm daily time tracker, inspired by Toggl Track and Superfocus, linked to Google Calendar.

**Open it:** https://thamistco.github.io/division-of-time/

- **One-tap tracking**: tap a category chip to start or switch; keys 1–9, S, F, N
- **Plan vs actual**: your Google Calendar beside what you actually did; log meetings and clinics in one tap; striped untracked gaps you can fill
- **Focus sessions** (25 / 50 / 90 min) with a focus rating and a "next step" note to cut attention residue
- **Forgotten-timer guard**: keep, stop at when you left, or discard
- **Goals** per category (at least / at most), deep-focus and free-time gauges
- **Shutdown review**: rate the day, note what stole time, set tomorrow's one big thing and send it to Google Calendar
- **Insights**: hours per day, categories vs goals, focus heatmap and best window, switches per hour, plan kept, work spillover, review streaks
- Optional write-back of tracked time to a Google calendar of your choice (as "free" time)
- Works offline, installs to your home screen, dark mode. Entries stay on your device; export JSON/CSV anytime.

Research behind the nudges: Harkin et al. 2016 (progress monitoring), Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 (implementation intentions), Buehler 1994 (planning fallacy), Leroy 2009 (attention residue), Sharif et al. 2021 (free time and wellbeing), RescueTime focus data.
